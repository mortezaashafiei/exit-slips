Selected research projects

CPTu thrust mitigation in glauconite sand — PIGS Joint Industry Project

  • Field evaluation of six CPTu tooling configurations in the Hornerstown and Navesink glauconitic formations.
  • 48 mm friction reducers with upper-bevel water jetting (6–7 L/min) extended penetration from ≈14 m to 23.5 m; standard friction reducers reduced rod thrust by 50–80%.

Calibration-chamber modeling of pile setup in clay

  • Pressurized consolidation chamber and miniature model pile instrumented with 3 piezometers and 3 strain gauges.
  • 21 static load tests (1 hour to 90 days) at OCR 1.2 and 4.8; separated EPWP dissipation (+287% in < 3 days) from thixotropic aging (+28.8% beyond 3 days).

3D particle sphericity from 2D images via sequential sieving

  • Empirical relations linking 2D projection circularity to 3D sphericity and Zingg ratios, validated with X-ray micro-CT.
